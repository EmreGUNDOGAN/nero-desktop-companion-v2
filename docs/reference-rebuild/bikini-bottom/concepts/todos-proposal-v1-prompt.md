Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE NEW full-page visual design proposal for the Turkish Nero desktop app's SpongeBob / Bikini Bottom TASKS page ("İşler"). This page has not been designed before. Only design this one page, no badges page and no montage.
Input image 1 is the already approved three-page SpongeBob theme: use it strictly as the reference for illustration style, palette, rounded navy title typography, cream paper texture, yellow sponge-pattern header and scalloped navigation. Input image 2 is the real approved Notes implementation: match its proportions, legible fonts and crafted illustrated paper surfaces. They are style references, not edit targets.
Format: one tall portrait app window viewed straight-on, full page from header to seabed footer, all objects completely visible, no surrounding desktop, no device frame. Detailed polished 2D hand-painted cartoon illustration with subtle paper grain and clean dark navy ink, faithful to the references. No photorealism, generic gradients, muddy 3D rendering or disconnected image patches.
Keep the shared header and menu nearly identical to the references: "Nero", flower mark, "Bikini Bottom", gear/pin/minimize/close controls; six evenly spaced illustrated menu icons with labels "Bugün", "Notlar", "İşler", "Sayaç", "Rozetler", "Bütçe". Select only İşler in the same coral pill. The letters İ and ş must be correct and as bold as the other menu labels.
Below navigation, a cream-paper title strip "İşler". A modest, richly illustrated underwater hero beneath it: the recognizable wooden Krusty Krab restaurant exterior with nautical windows and flags, SpongeBob holding a small order pad, cyan sea, restrained bubbles/flower motif. Small cream speech balloon "Bir iş daha hazır!" Keep the hero modest enough that the functional task board remains the dominant part of the page.
Under the hero: a warm parchment control strip with readable small summary "Bugün: 3 iş · yaklaşık 1 sa 20 dk" and "1 işin süresi belirtilmedi". A compact row of search "İşlerde ara…", dropdown "Tüm öncelikler", dropdown "Etiket". Below that, a long pale new-task input "Bir sonraki küçük adım…" and the familiar coral + button, then a small collapsed "Süre ve hatırlatma" control.
Main object: a nautical wooden order clipboard with curved wood grain frame, two small brass clips and cream lined-paper interior. No office whiteboard. It contains two clearly divided task columns "Bugün (3)" and "Sonra (2)". Keep all text readable and aligned, generous paper padding, subtle paper edge shadows; headings use the same rounded heavy navy font as the references, body text the same clear condensed sans-serif.
Today tasks, each its own soft cream or pale blue paper card:
1. checkbox, "Sunumu bitir", red chip "Öncelikli", blue chip "İş"; beneath "Plan: 45 dk · Çalışılan: 20 dk", a small green progress strip "2/4 adım". At right a small circular play control and ellipsis.
2. checkbox, "Kitap bölümünü oku", green chip "Kişisel"; "Plan: 35 dk"; play and ellipsis.
3. checkbox, "E-postaları yanıtla", blue chip "İş"; "Süre belirtilmedi"; play and ellipsis.
Later tasks on soft pastel yellow/pink paper cards:
1. checkbox, "Hafta sonunu planla", green chip "Kişisel", lavender chip "Planlama"; "Plan: 20 dk"; play and ellipsis.
2. checkbox, "Kitapçıya uğra", green chip "Kişisel"; "Plan: 30 dk"; play and ellipsis.
Below the columns, still within the lined-paper board, a compact collapsible section "Bugün tamamlananlar · 1 iş", one checked smaller quiet row "Dosyaları düzenle" with blue "İş" chip and date "8 Eki". Add a discreet bottom hint "Sıralamak için sürükle · Bugün ve Sonra arasında taşı".
Below the clipboard a matching illustrated wooden/paper archive drawer "Arşivler · 2 iş", collapsed. Finish with the same sandy underwater border, green seaweed, small purple rocks, colorful coral and complete "Bikini Bottom" wooden sign used in the approved Notes theme. Avoid duplicating characters, cropped flowers or objects, excessive decorations, blank unexplained buttons, tiny text, repeated headings and invented extra app features.
All Turkish copy must be spelled accurately, horizontal baselines aligned, task tag colors visibly distinct, native-looking interactive controls unobscured. This is a cohesive production design reference that we will later extract into individual assets and code.
