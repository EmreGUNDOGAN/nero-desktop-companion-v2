## Üretim metni

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE NEW full-page visual design proposal for the Turkish Nero desktop app SpongeBob / Bikini Bottom ACHIEVEMENTS page, "Rozetler". This page has not been designed before. Design only this page, no montage.
Input image 1 is the user-approved TASKS design: match its finely crafted 2D cartoon illustration style, yellow sponge-pattern header, scalloped six-item navigation, navy rounded heavy title font, clear condensed body font, cream textured paper, turquoise underwater scene and sandy footer. Input image 2 is the original approved three-page theme, also a STYLE reference. They are references, not edit targets.
Output: one tall portrait app window, straight-on full page from complete header to complete seabed footer, around the same proportions as image 1. High visual polish, legible Turkish, restrained warm paper grain, crisp dark outlines, warm cozy handmade materials. Not photorealistic or generic flat dashboard.
Header should be almost exactly the Tasks reference: "Nero" and flower, "Bikini Bottom", gear/pin/minimize/close; navigation "Bugün", "Notlar", "İşler", "Sayaç", "Rozetler", "Bütçe" with same illustrated icons. Only Rozetler selected in the coral rounded tab. Ensure İ, ş and all menu labels are bold and correct, all icons complete.
Under navigation, a modest cyan underwater hero showing SpongeBob proudly holding a shiny golden star award, with Patrick beside him, both fully formed and happy, sea flowers and bubbles, colorful small coral and sandy base. Include title "Rozetler" in the same large rounded heavy navy lettering. Small cream speech balloon "Küçük adımlar, büyük hatıralar." The illustration is secondary to the collection.
Below, a beautiful cream parchment collection heading surface, complete rounded paper edges and flower ornament: title "Küçük adımların hatıraları", right-aligned "5 / 100". Below it a neat small progress strip, five percent filled, no fake 100% completion. Under that a single row of four equal, readable paper buttons: "Yaygın", "Sıradışı", "Nadir", "Efsanevi". Yaygın selected coral; others pale cream. No text overlaps or paper cuts. Do NOT show a locked secret category.
Then a compact aligned category row "Yaygın" and "5 / 25".
Main collection: a nautical WOODEN DISPLAY BOARD matching Tasks wood grain, with a clean cream-paper backing and six equal achievement cards in a 3-column by 2-row grid. Generous consistent gutters, all full cards visible. Not an Office cork board, no photorealistic props. Each card soft textured cream or pale blue/yellow paper with subtly irregular rounded edge, small brass or shell fastening at top, a centered bespoke colorful cartoon collectible illustration, navy heavy title, readable short body, and small date on unlocked cards. Each illustration must be a separate clear object, no rectangles pasted into scene. Five cards have small green circular checkmarks in upper right. Sixth locked card shows a neat lock instead, faded illustration, no date. Titles, body copy and icons all fit with breathing room.
Card 1: title "Bir yerden başlamak lazım", body "İlk işini bitirdin.", centered golden nautical star medal, date "8 Eki".
Card 2: "Isınma turları", "Toplam 10 iş bitirdin.", small Krusty Krab order-pad with a tasteful number 10 seal, "8 Eki".
Card 3: "Verimli gün", "Bir günde 5 iş bitirdin.", colorful Krabby Patty on a small plate, "8 Eki".
Card 4: "İlk odak", "Bir sayacı sonuna kadar tamamladın.", miniature brass porthole stopwatch with cyan glass, "8 Eki".
Card 5: "Alışkanlık başlıyor", "3 gün üst üste Nero'ya geldin.", small three-day paper calendar with tiny sea flower, "8 Eki".
Card 6 locked: "Aklımda tutarım demedin", "İlk notunu oluştur.", faded seashell-and-pencil notebook, small circular navy lock. Card readable but quieter. No fake awarded date.
The six cards are examples from a scrollable larger collection; do not invent pagination, purchasing, currency or claimed reward controls. Finish the wooden board naturally.
Footer full sandy underwater border matching Tasks and Notes: green seaweed, small purple rocks, colorful coral and complete "Bikini Bottom" wooden sign. Do not crop flowers, fasteners, characters, badges or sign. No overlapping title/filter strip, extra blank controls, repeated labels, tiny illegible copy, second Nero title, watermarks or surrounding desktop. Keep font style strictly faithful to the references. This is a production design reference for extraction into separate raster assets followed by native code implementation.
